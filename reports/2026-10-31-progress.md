# Progress report, 2026-10-31

What changed in the method between the first debates (July 2026) and 2026-10-31, and whether the
user's ratings moved. The numbers come from `scorecards/`, the merged pull requests in this repo,
and the moderator's private notes, of which only counts are given here.

## Summary

- **Ratings.** Five meetings have a 1 to 5 rating so far: 2, 4, 4, 2, 4 (average 3.2). The one
  rated round run in the old two-pass format scored 2; the four rated meetings since the meeting
  format average 3.5.
- **The cleanest comparison** is two idea meetings of the same kind. Idea meeting 2 (T010) scored 2:
  the user found the ideas shallow, as if nobody had thought them through. One bundle of rules
  changed before idea meeting 3 (T011), which scored 4: the user said the quality of the candidates
  went up. The bundle was "think before you weed" (benchmark, at least five private drafts, a
  deepening turn before any objection).
- **Fact errors** (a persona misreading a fact, per persona per round) fell from 0.29 in two-pass
  rounds to 0.19 in meetings, 0.10 in the first idea meetings, and 0 after the think-before-weed
  rules.
- **Feedback loop.** The user made 28 remarks about how meetings run. All 28 are now rules, in
  `METHOD.md`, the roster, `lessons/LESSONS.md`, or the private local settings. None is pending.
- **Adoptions.** Five ideas were adopted from meetings held between 2026-10-28 and 2026-10-31, and
  building has started on four.
- **Not yet known.** The fix for the software-only skew (2026-10-31) is being tested by idea
  meeting 5 (T013), which is still running. Its opening statements put 9 of 10 new ideas outside
  software, against almost none of the 30 new ideas in idea meetings 2 to 4. It has no rating yet.

## What improved

### The shape of a round
- **Problem.** With one proposal pass and one rebuttal each, most ideas were never challenged but
  still counted as survivors. The design the user saw was the moderator's synthesis, never argued in
  the room, and personas filled in the user's private facts by guessing.
- **Change (2026-10-23).** The meeting format.
- **Effect.** Rated meetings since then average 3.5, against 2 for the one rated two-pass round.

### Context before the meeting
- **Problem.** T001 scored 2: the cast did not know what kind of room it was or who would answer.
- **Change (2026-10-26).** The five-item context.
- **Effect.** The next rated meeting, T007, scored 4.

### The cast
- **Problem.** All specialists, with no creative or trend voice; insiders who were partisan in a
  comparison; nobody who would build the ideas; the same personas returning and pulling the same way.
- **Change (2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31).** Seats by role (concept, trend, builder, critic, open), a
  rotation rule, adopted authors sitting out, a culture trend scout, and outside-software personas
  first for the open seat.
- **Effect.** The roster grew from 24 to 33 personas. T013 is the first idea meeting with a
  persona from outside software and a non-developer trend persona.

### The depth of ideas
- **Problem.** T010 scored 2. Each persona opened with its first two thoughts, and six turns only
  weeded them: seven of ten new ideas fell to one-line answers from the user. Every idea left was a
  fix or an automation of the user's current tools, which the user called skill work, not an idea.
- **Change (2026-10-31).** The user's bar for a good idea goes into the context: thinking deeply,
  benchmarking, analyzing the material and deliberating. Each persona benchmarks and writes at least
  five drafts before the opening and brings the two least obvious. The first turn deepens other
  personas' ideas, and the moderator deals every idea on the table so each is deepened once.
- **Effect.** T011 scored 4 with two adoptions. In T011 four of five deepenings went to two ideas;
  with the deal, T012 deepened all 18 of its ideas once each.

### The software-only skew
- **Problem.** Idea meetings 2 to 4 put 30 new ideas on the table, almost all software or AI. The
  moderator had filled the context with software facts (19 of 26 in T012, and all five trend facts
  from developer surveys), and nine of the ten personas on the idea-meeting seat lists were
  software jobs.
- **Change (2026-10-31).** The moderator stops gathering material; personas read the user's own
  material, browse and benchmark for themselves, with a source for each fact. Earlier answers and
  adopted ideas enter only as what not to propose again. The pool is shown after the openings.
- **Effect (preliminary, no rating).** 9 of the 10 opening ideas in T013 are not software.

### How the moderator talks to the user
- **Problem.** On 2026-10-30 alone the user had to ask five times what something meant or say it
  had been misread: a handle the moderator coined for a meeting, a report in the room's own terms, a
  question that named a feature without saying what it did, and two misread instructions.
- **Change.** Name a meeting or idea in full the first time; open every report with two or three
  plain sentences; ask about a feature by what it did; state a reading of ambiguous words before
  acting; when an answer could decide an idea, also ask whether the user would use it.
- **Effect.** None of the 11 remarks on 2026-10-31 was of this kind; they were about the quality and
  range of ideas. Two days is too short to call this settled.

### After the meeting
- A report and a reading page at every close (2026-10-30).
- A candidate pool: 15 candidates, 5 adopted, 4 dropped by the user so far.
- Ideas withdrawn because of the user's answers are listed apart, so the user can revive them. One
  was revived the next day and adopted.

## Limits

- Five ratings, on different topics, cannot pin any change on a single rule. T010 added rules and
  still dropped from 4 to 2; finding why (shallow ideas) led to the change that brought T011 to 4.
- Ten rounds have no rating: T002 rounds 3 to 6, T003 rounds 1 and 2, T004, T006, T008 and T012.
  T002 is the only thread with both two-pass and meeting rounds on one topic, so it is the only
  direct test of the 2026-10-23 lesson, which asked for exactly that comparison. It is still open.
- The share of ideas used depends on the kind of meeting: shaping one project used five of ten,
  while an idea meeting that picks one or two from many uses one or two. It is not compared across
  kinds.
- The method grew fast: `METHOD.md` went from 65 lines (2026-10-14) to 186, and longer briefs make
  meetings slower and costlier. No harm has shown yet; the 2026-07-22 lesson on short context still
  applies.
- Personas still sometimes return only a status line (every persona once in T004). Asking once to
  re-output recovers the content.
